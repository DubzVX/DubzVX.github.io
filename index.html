ABOUT ME

Howdy, Fox! I'm Rémi — Lead Threat Hunter driving intelligence-led operations to detect and neutralize advanced cyber threats before they cause damage.

My work spans the full kill chain: hunting APTs across enterprise environments, reverse engineering malware to understand what it's really doing, and turning raw observations into detection rules the SOC can actually use. I work primarily with Splunk and SentinelOne., build automation around threat intelligence, and track adversary groups from first beacon to attribution. When I'm not on a hunt, I'm tearing apart malware samples.

I publish my research, detection rules, and playbooks as open-source to contribute to the defensive security community.
